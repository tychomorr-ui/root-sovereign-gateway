# ROOT Monarch Federation Blueprint

**Status:** Implemented foundation; expansion remains approval-gated.  
**Scope:** ROOT’s governed directory and issuer topology. This blueprint does not authorize data sale, behavioral tracking, surveillance, public exposure of operator identities, or any claim of partnership without written authorization.

## Purpose

ROOT uses a **3–6–9 Monarch federation** to distribute reviewed operational responsibility without treating the network as a universal authority. Nodes carry narrow, reviewable mandates. They may support source-verified directory stewardship, issuer review, and recovery processes only within their assigned scope.

> **Operating rule:** A node extends accountable capability; it never overrides member consent, creates public identity exposure, or converts provenance signals into a claim of universal truth.

The private registry contains all eighteen defined mandates. **A1, the NEXINUS Business Anchor, is the only active anchor.** Its assigned operator is retained privately in the database, with a review due date and activity status. The other seventeen entries are planning records only: they do not have public operator identities, physical locations, or authority to publish resources.

The owner-supplied business member credential was privately checked against the A1 assignment on **August 11, 2026**. The credential resolved to the same internal user record assigned to A1, whose state is active and has a future review date. ## Directory Expansion Protocol

Regional resources are not added by assumption. Each proposed service or emergency resource must be independently sourced from an official provider, a government service, or another authoritative primary source. A record must include its source URL, publication state, last review date, next review date, and an active-node assignment before it may be published.

The initial geographic priority is **Ukiah and Mendocino County**, followed by broader California coverage, the United States, and then an approval-gated international federation. Planned nodes are not a reason to create placeholder contacts or fictional providers.

## Member and Consent Boundary

Membership, attestations, permissions, and private records remain member-controlled. A service may receive only the declared proof result needed for eligibility; it must not receive the underlying Truth Talk content, attestations, personal records, or consent history unless the member expressly grants a compatible scope. Emergency location requests use one-time consent and do not create persistent location history.

## Verification Record

The implementation is covered by automated policy and authorization tests. In the issuer experience, the dedicated `/issuer/nodes` route renders the active A1 anchor, seventeen planned mandates, registration controls, and a chronological node-audit ledger. A live authenticated acceptance action remains a separate governance sign-off before operational operator or directory expansion occurs.

## Expansion Gate

Before adding a new active node, operator, partnership reference, or regional resource, ROOT requires a documented approval packet containing the proposed scope, operator/member identification, conflict disclosure, authority basis, source evidence where applicable, review cadence, and a revocation or suspension path. No implied partnership, invented contact, or unreviewed regional coverage may enter the published directory.
