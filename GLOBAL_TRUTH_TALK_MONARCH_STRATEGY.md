# ROOT, Truth Talk, and the Monarch Protocol

## Executive position

The ambition is powerful: **ROOT becomes the sovereign gateway, Truth Talk becomes the public evidence surface, and Monarch becomes the recursive coordination protocol that connects people, proofs, resources, and accountable operators.** The viable version is not omniscient or omnipotent. It is **high-integrity, transparent about uncertainty, globally federated, and difficult to capture**.

ROOT should not promise “only truth” in an absolute sense. It should promise that every material claim can carry a visible trail: who made it, what type of claim it is, what source or media evidence is attached, what integrity checks succeeded, who reviewed it, what disputes exist, and whether it has been corrected or withdrawn. This distinction is essential: the W3C explicitly notes that cryptographic verification of a credential does **not** itself establish the truth of the encoded claim; the verifier must still assess issuer, proof, subject, claims, and context. [1]

| Product promise | What ROOT can honestly guarantee | What ROOT must not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Provenance | A source, issuer, file digest, signature, or media history was declared and checked | The underlying claim is universally true |
| Identity | A creator or issuer used a verified account or credential | The creator is morally trustworthy or always correct |
| Review | A named review process occurred and remains inspectable | A review makes information beyond dispute |
| Correction | A correction, dispute, withdrawal, or update is permanently linked to the original record | The original record never existed |
| Resources | A directory entry was checked against a stated official or authoritative source at a recorded time | A provider is currently available, suitable, or safe for every person |

## The Monarch protocol

The Monarch name works best as a **biomimetic systems pattern**. North American monarch migration is a multi-generation journey that depends on distributed orientation, seasonal state, environmental cues, and convergent destinations. The biological analogy is useful because the system persists across generations without a single butterfly holding the entire route. Monarchs have evidence-supported sun- and magnetic-compass mechanisms; research also connects cryptochrome 1 to light-dependent magnetic sensing in monarchs. [4] [5] This is not evidence that human institutions can access quantum entanglement, prediction, or omniscience.

> **Monarch Protocol definition:** A recursive, privacy-preserving coordination protocol in which every participating node publishes its capability, evidence requirements, consent boundaries, review cadence, correction route, and revocation endpoint.

The operational pattern is **sense → attest → route → act → receipt → review → correct**. Each loop produces a bounded record that can guide the next loop without consolidating every person’s data into a central behavioral profile.

| Monarch component | Function in ROOT | Concrete first implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Local signal | A member, operator, publisher, or resource identifies a need or claim | Structured Truth Talk post or resource submission |
| Capability manifest | A node declares what it can do and what it needs | Versioned service or node policy record |
| Consent edge | The member authorizes a narrowly scoped exchange | Existing ROOT consent receipt |
| Evidence packet | A claim or resource carries supporting material | Source URLs, file digest, issuer, timestamps, and review status |
| Route receipt | ROOT explains why an action was permitted or denied | Capability Graph decision receipt |
| Review clock | Every authority and resource is rechecked on schedule | Existing operator, service, and emergency review dates |
| Correction path | A challenge or update changes status without erasing history | Dispute, correction, revocation, and withdrawal workflow |

## Truth Talk as the public evidence layer

Truth Talk can have the immediacy of short-form social media while remaining unlike TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, Reddit, or Nostr in one decisive respect: **the attention layer never hides the evidence layer**. Swipeable media is the entry point; provenance, context, and correction are always one interaction away.

### Claim packet

Every post should become an immutable-but-correctable **claim packet** with the following fields. A creator may leave fields blank, but ROOT must show what is missing rather than silently implying completeness.

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Claim type | `report`, `firsthand account`, `opinion`, `analysis`, `correction`, or `question` |
| Author binding | A pseudonymous or identified account binding, displayed at the author’s chosen privacy level |
| Evidence links | Source URLs, documents, public records, or cited witnesses |
| Integrity receipt | Local file digest or C2PA-compatible provenance if available |
| Geographic scope | Optional and coarse by default; never a hidden continuous location history |
| Time scope | When the reported event happened, separately from publication time |
| Review state | Individual TruthOK signals, reviewer notes, dispute state, and correction chain |
| Harm designation | Content sensitivity and distribution controls, not a truth judgment |

C2PA provides a technical framework for the source and history of media, including content credentials and attestations. [2] NIST similarly identifies provenance tracking, synthetic-content labeling, detection, software testing, and auditing as relevant technical risk-reduction approaches. [3] ROOT should adopt these tools as evidence inputs, while never treating them as automatic arbiters of truth.

### Three viewing modes

The feed can support the user’s desire for broad visibility without forcing unwanted graphic material on everyone.

| Mode | Default audience | Distribution rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | All users | Non-graphic presentation, credible-source context prioritized, sensitive media blurred or excluded |
| **Context** | Users who opt in | Adds disputed, developing, and graphic-context reports with clear warnings and source provenance |
| **Explicit** | Age-appropriate users who make an affirmative choice | Strong warnings, no autoplay, no monetization incentive, no recommendation amplification, and regional legal restrictions honored |

No mode should use engagement-maximizing ranking for violent or traumatic content. A post’s reach should be determined by member choice, declared relevance, and evidence context—not outrage metrics. Content involving imminent harm, exploitation, non-consensual imagery, or unlawful material requires escalation, reporting, and access controls rather than merely a viewer toggle.

## From “all providers” to a global resource federation

An all-source directory is achievable as a **federation**, not as a single omniscient database. ROOT should aim for comprehensive discoverability while representing uncertainty honestly. Each regional resource has an explicit source authority, verification date, coverage boundary, contact route, review deadline, and status.

| Directory tier | Source of record | ROOT treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Tier A: official public source | Government, recognized emergency service, or provider-owned official website | Eligible for direct publication after current-source review |
| Tier B: authoritative civic directory | Established public-service network or accredited regional directory | Eligible with source attribution and shorter review cycle |
| Tier C: local verified organization | Community provider with identity and public contact verification | Publish only through a Regional Directory Steward and periodic review |
| Tier D: member-submitted lead | A submitted but not yet verified resource | Keep private to the verification queue; never present as a verified directory entry |

The correct launch sequence is **Ukiah and Mendocino County → California → United States → China → regional federation**. This is not a retreat from global ambition. It is how ROOT creates a replicable verification packet before expansion. Every region needs local language support, authoritative source mapping, local operator accountability, review cadences, and lawful content rules. A global directory that does not preserve these boundaries becomes inaccurate quickly.

## Operator architecture

The existing First Executive should approve people only after they hold a ROOT member code and accept a narrow mandate. The first expansion should not use generic “admin” access.

| Role | Primary authority | Required evidence | Recommended first scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Directory Steward | Verify and maintain resources | Public-source review, regional mandate, conflict disclosure | Mendocino County or a defined U.S. region |
| Issuer | Issue narrow attestations | Written issuance policy and auditable mandate | Specific evidence or service domain |
| Incident Reviewer | Handle disputes, corrections, and revocations | Conflict disclosure and escalation training | One content or directory area |
| Translation Steward | Preserve source meaning across languages | Language competence and source citation practice | English–Chinese or other named language pair |

## Partnership accuracy

The message becomes stronger, not weaker, when it is exact. ROOT can say that it was built using a given tool, that it shares a stated design aspiration, or that it welcomes aligned collaboration. ROOT must **not** say that any company, product, or person is a partner, sponsor, affiliate, or endorser without their written authorization. The same applies to unverified claims about another company’s ownership, strategy, or relationship with third parties.

The credible partnership approach is a concise proposal: working proof of ROOT, its consent and governance model, a narrowly scoped pilot, an explicit request, and no implied endorsement. This protects ROOT’s reputation and the user’s leverage.

## Required decisions before building the next increment

1. Name the first two approved operators by their ROOT member codes, labels, and desired narrow roles.
2. Confirm whether the first regional directory packet should prioritize **Ukiah/Mendocino County** categories such as shelter, food, health, safety, and legal support.
3. Choose whether **Monarch Protocol** should be a public product name, an internal architecture name, or an optional brand within Monarch OS.
4. Confirm the initial Truth Talk distribution policy: Standard-only at launch, or Standard plus Context with an Explicit mode deferred until its safety and age controls are tested.
