# Project Reclaim Research Record

**Research date:** August 11, 2026  
**Scope:** Initial ROOT integration for Mendocino County and Lake County, California.  
**Publication rule:** A source link is not a partnership claim. ROOT may list a source-governed opportunity or resource only as a pointer to its named public provider. It must not claim to employ, place, diagnose, fund, represent, or act for any provider without written authorization.

## White Paper Grounding

The user-supplied *Project Reclaim: A National Framework for Human Restoration, Version 1.0 Draft* defines the program as a coordinated human-restoration framework focused on stability, opportunity, education, employment, housing, and community. Its stated pillars are **Health, Housing, Work, Education, Community, and Independence**. The draft establishes participant consent, data minimization, transparency, security, and human oversight as privacy and ethics principles, and identifies an initial sequence of foundation, pilot, regional expansion, statewide network, and national framework.

ROOT should therefore serve as a **member-controlled coordination and source-verification surface**, not as an automated case manager, benefits eligibility engine, worker surveillance layer, or replacement for human professionals. Project Reclaim participation must be opt-in and private by default. A member’s interest in an opportunity must never be disclosed to an employer, provider, county agency, or partner without a separate explicit grant.

## Initial Program Design Implications

Project Reclaim can begin in ROOT as a member-private **opportunity and restoration navigator** with three controlled layers. The first is a public-source directory of explicitly reviewed community resources and opportunities. The second is a private member-interest ledger in which members can record interest, skills, constraints, or support goals for themselves. The third is a consent-controlled referral or introduction action that cannot disclose anything until the member approves a specific recipient, purpose, and field set.

The initial regional rollout should include **Mendocino County and Lake County** as labels, with each entry carrying a county, Project Reclaim pillar, source URL, verification date, review due date, publication state, and current availability note. ROOT must refrain from seeding jobs, claiming open positions, or inferring government or provider partnership without current primary-source evidence.
