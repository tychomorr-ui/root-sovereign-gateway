# ROOT Launch Readiness Checklist

## Governance

- The First Executive has reviewed the issuer approval policy and is the only active administrator until additional operators are formally approved.
- Each operator has a role, approval date, status, approver, and conflict-of-interest confirmation.
- Suspended operators have no remaining operational access.

## Directories

- Every published service includes a current official source URL and a plainly stated minimum eligibility requirement.
- Every published emergency resource includes a current official source URL, contact route, region label, and review date.
- No fictional, placeholder, stale, or unverified provider is published.

## Member experience

- A member can update their private display name, create and revoke a consent receipt, publish a Truth Talk claim with provenance signals, and export their own record.
- A service receives a minimum-proof result, not the member’s evidence details.
- Emergency lookup requires explicit one-time location consent and never creates persistent location history.

## Incident handling

- Immediate danger routes users to local emergency services.
- Directory inaccuracies are unpublished first and investigated second.
- Evidence disputes are recorded, scoped, and reviewed by an active Incident Reviewer or administrator.
