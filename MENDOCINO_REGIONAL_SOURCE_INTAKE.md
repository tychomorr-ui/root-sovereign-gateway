## Publication safeguards

The two records marked eligible are appropriate for addition only because they are described in the source’s own terms, link directly to an official government page, and remain assigned to the active A1 mandate. ROOT must not claim that it provides crisis care, determines program eligibility, dispatches emergency help, or partners with these agencies.

For urgent, life-threatening emergencies, the application must preserve the source-agency instruction to call **911**. The behavioral-health record should distinguish the 24/7 crisis line from the separate non-crisis warm line to avoid misrouting a person seeking immediate help. [1] [4]

## Review cadence

The recommended next review is **November 11, 2026** (90 days) or sooner if a source page changes, the provider requests a correction, or a member reports a routing issue. Publication should include its source URL, last review date, next review date, and the active A1 assignment. No resource should be published without all four fields.
